02   Choose conversion

Choose what you are converting from and to.

Tell the tool where the coordinates are expected to be, then choose the simple From and To formats. If you do not know the original format, leave From on Detect automatically. Exact datums, zones, and EPSG codes are optional.

Alberta is used to catch common longitude sign issues. For example, 114° can be safely treated as 114° W only when the matching negative longitude falls in Alberta.
